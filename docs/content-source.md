## Editorial choices and boundaries

- The main heading is “Anxiety and trauma therapy in Santa Monica, CA.” Local terms appear naturally in supporting text without repeating the full heading in every section.
- The displayed location is “Santa Monica, CA 90401.” The malformed street address in the supplied material is omitted rather than corrected by guessing.
- The three service groups are anxiety and panic; trauma; burnout and perfectionism. They organize profile-supported concerns without adding new specialties.
- Warm, supportive language is descriptive. It must not imply guaranteed recovery, a specific result, or a prescribed number of sessions.
- FAQs cover adult clients, approaches, in-person and California telehealth formats, and carefully paced trauma work. Information absent from the profile, including fees and availability, is not invented.
- Consultation buttons open a clearly labeled assignment demo. No appointments are booked, no form collects personal details, and no booking success message is simulated.
- The footer identifies the practice as a fictional assignment concept. It does not present this preview as an operational therapy practice.

## Visual sources and intentional changes

- The clone at `/original` follows the reference section order, asymmetric rectangular photography, generous spacing, oval buttons, and responsive placement.
- Cormorant Garamond, Allura, and Mulish are the approved clone font substitutes. Exact matching to the reference fonts is an intentional exception.
- The redesign at `/` uses Fraunces for headings and DM Sans for body text. Its palette is ocean `#234C5A`, pale blue `#DCE7E8`, sand `#F3EEE5`, bronze `#927047`, ink `#23383F`, and white `#FCFCFA`.
- The portrait and both office photographs come from the supplied profile. Other redesign imagery uses coordinated coastal and natural-detail photography; the final asset sources and licensing records belong in the README.
- Our Office is the one additional homepage section. FAQs and consultation details use dialogs so they do not add extra sections to the preserved page sequence.
